# 3D Print Enclosure

## Requirements

* Enclosure must clip onto the shaft
* Enclosure must contain the PCB and battery
* Enclosure must snap onto the shaft
* USBC is not required to be accessible
* LED is not required to be visible
* PLA filament
* Mounted near grip of golf club
* The Y axis of the PCB should point up the shaft

## Dimensions

* Shaft mounting location diameter: 13mm
* PCB (and battery): X 45mm, Y 70mm, Z 30mm
